Shipping & Returns

Merch you see on this site is sold at therapytoaster.myshopify.com (Mr. Toasty's Shop) and printed on demand by Printful. This page is a plain-language summary of the store's shipping and refund policies, so you can read them without leaving therapytoaster.com.

The official policies are the ones posted on the Shopify store. If anything here and there ever differs, the store pages win:

  • Shipping Policy
  • Refund Policy
  • Terms of Service

Those store policies were last updated October 31, 2026. For order questions, email Mr.Toasty@TherapyToaster.com and include your order number.

Shipping

  • Production. Every order is printed on demand. Production usually takes 2–7 business days before your order ships.
  • Delivery. After production, carrier transit time is added. Rates and estimated delivery times are shown at checkout, based on your address.
  • Where we ship. We ship within the United States. International shipping is available to the countries offered at checkout. International orders may take longer. Customs duties, import taxes, or fees charged by your country are the buyer's responsibility unless checkout says otherwise.
  • Promos. This website may advertise promotions, such as free US shipping on orders of $69 or more. The amount you actually pay, including shipping, is the amount shown at Shopify checkout.
  • Tracking. You'll get an email with a tracking number when your order ships.
  • Multiple items. Items in one order may be printed at different facilities and arrive in separate packages.
  • Address check. Please double-check your shipping address. Email us right away if you spot a mistake. We can fix it only before your order goes into production.

Returns and refunds

Every item is made just for you. Tees, hats, and other made-to-order items are printed after you order, so the store can't take returns or exchanges for a change of heart, buyer's remorse, or a wrong size you selected. Please check the size chart before you order.

If we got it wrong, we'll make it right. If your item arrives misprinted, damaged, or defective, or you got the wrong item or size from what you ordered, email Mr.Toasty@TherapyToaster.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a clear photo of the problem (and of the label, for a wrong size). Once that's reviewed, you'll get a free replacement or a full refund to your original payment method, whichever you prefer. You won't need to send the item back unless you're asked to.

  • Lost packages. If tracking shows your order hasn't moved for a long time, or it says delivered but never arrived, email us and we'll work with the carrier. Orders lost because of a wrong address entered at checkout can be reshipped at your cost.
  • Cancellations. Production starts fast. Email as soon as possible. If the order hasn't gone into production yet, it can be cancelled and refunded in full. Once it's printing, it can't be cancelled.
  • Refund timing. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. How long they take to show up depends on your bank or card issuer.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the law where you live.
