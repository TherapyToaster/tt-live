Cookies

therapytoaster.com uses cookies and similar storage so the site can run, remember a few preferences, and load embeds. We don't use a cookie consent banner on this site right now. This page lists what we actually found. The full story is in our Privacy page.

Cookies this site sets

The site is hosted by Squarespace. In a live check, the browser received these cookies on therapytoaster.com:

  • crumb — a Squarespace security cookie.
  • ss_cvr and ss_cvt — Squarespace analytics cookies, used to understand visits to the site.

Squarespace may also collect technical information such as your IP address, browser, and the pages you visit. See Squarespace's privacy policy for their details.

Stored in your browser, not as cookies

The site also uses local storage and session storage on your device for things like the intro screen, sound settings, the radio mini-player, a Daily Glitch streak, the last known visit-count number, and ARTCADE game saves or high scores. Those stay in your browser.

Cookies and tools from other companies

Some pages load content from other services. Those services may set their own cookies or collect technical information when their content loads:

  • Twitter embeds and widgets, on Home and Coty's Space.
  • Pinterest widget logging, which set a Pinterest cookie in our check.
  • Chatango chat, on ARTCADE and Coty's Space. Chatango's embed also loaded Google Analytics.
  • YouTube videos and thumbnails, on pages such as Stickdeath and Crumb Radio.
  • Sociablekit, which powers a Twitter feed on Coty's Space.
  • Requests to Abacus (anonymous visit and play counters), GitHub Pages, jsDelivr, price APIs on Home (CoinGecko, CNBC, and Coinbase as a fallback), radio.co, and Suno, as described in the Privacy page.

If you follow a link to the Shopify store and check out, Shopify and its partners set their own cookies under the store's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can block cookies or clear stored site data in your browser settings. That may break embeds, counters, or saved game progress. Questions: Mr.Toasty@TherapyToaster.com.
