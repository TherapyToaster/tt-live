What Circle Check is

Circle Check is a private checkup for your X account. It is a read-only tool built on the X API. It snapshots your followers and following, then shows two lists: accounts you follow that stopped following you back, and accounts that liked, replied, reposted, or quoted your posts but that you don't follow yet.

What it will not do

Circle Check never posts, follows, unfollows, or blocks for you. You get two clear lists and decide who to follow or unfollow yourself. Every decision stays yours.

Your data

No data is sold or shared. Follower and following snapshots are stored only in your own Grok Bot. You can disconnect at any time.
