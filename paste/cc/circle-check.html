About

Circle Check is a private checkup for your X account. It snapshots your followers and following, then shows two lists: accounts you follow that stopped following you back, and accounts that liked, replied, reposted, or quoted your posts but that you don't follow yet. It never follows, unfollows, blocks, or posts for you. Every decision stays yours.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect

    Connect your X account in Grok Bot.

  2. 2

    Snapshot

    Circle Check snapshots your followers and following and reads engagement on your recent posts.

  3. 3

    You decide

    You get two clear lists and decide who to follow or unfollow yourself.
