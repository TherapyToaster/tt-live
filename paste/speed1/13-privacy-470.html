Privacy

You can browse therapytoaster.com without creating an account or giving us your name, email, or other personal information. We don't sell your data, and we don't ask you for personal info just to look around.

This is still a normal website. It uses cookies and some third-party tools so pages can load, counters can work, and embeds can play. Those tools may collect technical information, such as your IP address and browser. This page explains that honestly. It covers this website, not the merch checkout on Shopify, which has its own policy.

Who we are

This site is Therapy Toaster, run by Coty (Mr. Toasty). Questions or requests: Mr.Toasty@TherapyToaster.com.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't have a newsletter signup or a contact form on this site. If you want to reach us, email the address above.
  • Comments are turned off. There is no Disqus comment box.
  • We didn't add a separate Google Analytics tag to the site. (Some third-party embeds load their own analytics; those are listed below.)

Squarespace, cookies, and analytics

The site is hosted by Squarespace. Squarespace sets cookies and has built-in analytics and performance tools. In our check of the live site, the browser received:

  • crumb — a Squarespace security cookie.
  • ss_cvr and ss_cvt — Squarespace analytics cookies, used to understand visits to the site.

Squarespace may also collect technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit, and dates and times. How long they keep that, and what else they store, is described in Squarespace's privacy policy.

There is no cookie consent banner on this site right now. That setting is off in Squarespace. The cookies we found are listed in this section.

Squarespace's platform can include visitor accounts, a cart, and reCAPTCHA to protect forms. Merch checkout itself is on Shopify, not on this site. We did not find a working contact or signup form on the pages we checked, and reCAPTCHA did not load during that check.

Anonymous visit and play counters

A visit counter on the site, and play counters on ARTCADE, use a free public counter service called Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev), under the namespace therapytoaster. These increment simple numbers, such as a site-wide visit total and per-game play counts. They do not ask for your name or email.

Your browser still makes a normal request to Abacus, which typically includes your IP address and similar technical details. We can't see a list of visitors from that. The counter also saves the last known visit total in your browser so the number can still appear if Abacus is briefly down.

Scripts and files we host elsewhere

Some scripts, images, and games are served from GitHub Pages (therapytoaster.github.io/tt-live) and from the jsDelivr CDN. Those services may see a request from your browser when those files load. A status file on GitHub Pages is also checked so the site can show when a Twitter Space is live.

What stays in your browser

The site stores a few preferences on your device, such as whether you've seen the intro, sound settings, the radio mini-player, and a Daily Glitch streak. ARTCADE games may save progress, settings, or high scores in your browser. That information stays on your device. We don't collect those saves onto a server of ours.

If you email us

If you write to Mr.Toasty@TherapyToaster.com, we see whatever you send, including your email address, and we use it to reply and to help with orders or questions. We don't use a form on this site to collect that.

If you buy merch

Shirts and other merch are sold at therapytoaster.myshopify.com (Mr. Toasty's Shop), printed and shipped by Printful. If you buy something, Shopify, its payment processors, Printful, and the shipping carriers handle the order. They collect what they need for checkout, payment, printing, and delivery — for example your name, email, and shipping address. We may see order details so we can help with support.

Those purchases are covered by the store's own Privacy Policy, Shipping Policy, and Refund Policy, not only by this page.

Embeds and other services

Some pages pull in content from other companies. When those load, the other company may collect information under its own privacy policy — often including cookies, your IP address, and what was viewed. Here's what we found on the live site:

  • Twitter. Embedded posts and Twitter's widget script load on Home, Insider Threat and Ashley Savvie's page. Twitter may set cookies (we saw Cloudflare cookies from Twitter's domains).
  • YouTube. Videos are embedded on pages such as Stickdeath (using YouTube's privacy-enhanced player) and appear on Crumb Radio. Google / YouTube may collect data when those thumbnails, players, or videos load.
  • Chatango. Chat embeds appear on ARTCADE. If you use the chat, Chatango's terms and privacy policy apply, and you may be asked for a name. Chatango's embed also loaded Google Analytics during our check. That is Chatango's script, not a tag we added to the whole site.
  • Lichess. ARTCADE includes a Lichess TV iframe.
  • Pinterest. A Pinterest widget on the site sent a request to Pinterest's logging server and set a Pinterest cookie in our check.
  • Price tickers on Home. Live price quotes are fetched from public APIs at CoinGecko, CNBC, and (as a fallback in the page code) Coinbase. Those services may see a request from your browser.
  • Audio. Area 54 radio is streamed through radio.co. Some tracks and artwork are hosted by Suno.
  • Shopify images. Product photos on this site are often loaded from Shopify's CDN. That's for pictures, not checkout, unless you follow a link to the store.

The site also links out to other places, including TikTok, Twitch, Kick, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, TradingView, Google Maps (on Fat Coty's Food Reviews), and various games. Those sites have their own policies. We don't collect information from you just because a link is on the page; if you click through, you're on their site.
