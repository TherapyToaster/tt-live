For my wife, Ashley (@AshleySavvie).
23 years together, and the true artist in this house.

She was the artist first

Back in high school, before we were even dating, my wife painted three guys for me. Meet one of them below: pale round head, big dark eyes, and the most serious little frown you've ever seen.

I play around with glitches. My wife is the true artist.

23 years later

23 years later, after an accident that changed the course of my journey, I got her guys tattooed onto my arm, straight from the real painting she did.

Her signature came along too, inked right under them. Now her art goes everywhere I go.

High-school sweethearts

She painted the two of us back when we were high-school sweethearts. She painted me a clown in high school too (I call him the Sad Clown).

Fun fact: before this year, the last concert I'd been to was with my now-wife, when I was 15 or 16, back in like 2005.

The little things

She took a collector's box I was about to throw away and made something out of it for me. She's such a thoughtful person.

When she's in the back seat, my hand is right there for hers. And like my loving wife tells me: angles matter.
